# Scoring rubric — how to rank the week's signals

This rubric is intentionally tender-first because a published tender is the single strongest proof that a city is about to buy. Budget line items and strategic plans are next: they precede the tender by months but commit the money. Press and news are weakest on their own, but pair well with other signals.

The goal of scoring is to produce a defensible top-10 each week. The rubric is stackable (bonuses add), capped at 20. Scores exist to rank, not to gate — a 6-point signal in a Tier-1 country can absolutely beat a 10-point signal in Estonia.

## Base points — signal type

| Signal | Base points | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Published tender/RFP referencing digital engagement | **10** | Active procurement. They have budget, timeline, and a decision window. |
| Budget line item with specific € figure for participation/engagement | **7** | Committed money, not yet spent. Usually precedes a tender by 3–9 months. |
| Multi-year strategic plan commitment (no € yet) | **6** | Political commitment. Strongest predictor of future budget. |
| Council resolution to launch a participation initiative | **5** | Formal decision. Harder to walk back. |
| Mayor speech / press release announcing participation work | **5** | Political capital being spent. Usually followed by budget/RFP. |
| Local news / council minutes mention | **3** | Weakest direct signal. Needs corroboration to rank well. |
| New participation office or team being stood up (per press release) | **6** | Structural commitment — implies a platform decision will follow. |

Pick the highest applicable tier per signal; don't double-count a single event across types.

## Bonuses (stackable, each one applies once per signal)

| Bonus | Points | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Multi-source confirmation** — same signal in ≥2 independent sources within the 14-day window | +2 | Halves the false-positive rate. |
| **Population ≥ 500k** | +2 | Larger contracts, better references, faster sales cycle in practice for Go Vocal. |
| **National or regional capital** | +1 | Halo effect — capital wins drive regional wins. |
| **Tier 1 geography** (see below) | +2 | Matches where Go Vocal wins today. |
| **Tier 2 geography** | +1 | Matches where we are actively expanding. |
| **Signal dated in last 7 days** (vs days 8–14 of window) | +1 | Fresher = more runway to engage before competitors. |
| **Explicit budget ≥ €100k / $100k / £100k** | +2 | Qualifies as a Go Vocal-sized deal. |
| **Platform capability named** (ideation, participatory budgeting, deliberation, consultation, co-creation) | +1 | Better match to Go Vocal's core. Less risk of "they want something we don't do." |
| **Deal-acceleration (existing Salesforce active opp)** | +1 | Applied after CRM check — lets the AE move faster on a live deal. |
| **Active competitor mention** (Cap Collectif, Decidim, CitizenLab, Granicus, etc. named in the source) | +2 | Strong competitive-swap opportunity; see `competitor-battlecard` skill for the positioning note. |

Cap total at **20 points**. Anything scoring ≥ 15 is exceptional — DM Wietse before the Tuesday Notion push.

## Geography tiers (from Go Vocal's ICP framework)

Grounded in Wietse's ICP/lead-scoring Notion doc and refined for intent-signal purposes.

### Tier 1 — White Glove (+2)
The markets where Go Vocal already wins on home turf.
- **United States**: California, Texas, Massachusetts, New York, Washington, Colorado
- **Canada**: English Canada (especially Ontario and BC)
- **United Kingdom**: all 4 nations
- **France**: all regions
- **Belgium — Flanders**
- **Netherlands**: all 12 provinces

### Tier 2 — Target (+1)
Active expansion markets.
- **United States**: all other states except Tier 1 and unlikely buyers
- **Canada**: all other provinces + Quebec (French-language)
- **Belgium — Wallonia, Brussels-Capital**
- **Ireland**
- **Germany**: NRW, Bayern, Baden-Württemberg, Berlin, Hamburg — urban and well-resourced
- **Nordics**: Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Finland (all)
- **Australia + New Zealand**

### Tier 3 — Volume (+0)
OECD democratic markets where we have fewer references but would take a good deal.
- **Spain**, **Portugal**, **Italy**, **Austria**, **Switzerland**, **Luxembourg**
- **Germany**: other states
- **Eastern EU**: Poland, Czech Republic, Estonia, Lithuania, Latvia, Slovenia, Slovakia

### Tier 4 — Non-target (exclude)
Outside scope. Drop the signal entirely even if everything else looks great.
- Non-democratic regimes
- Cities < 100k population unless regional/national capital
- Non-OECD geographies

## Fit-score modifiers (applied after base + bonuses)

From Go Vocal's ICP framework — these are multiplicative weights that adjust the score up or down based on account fit quality.

| Modifier | Multiplier | When to apply |
|---|---|---|
| Org profile = Local government | ×1.0 | Default. |
| Org profile = Regional/state/federal government | ×0.85 | Usable but lower fit. |
| Org profile = Vertical (healthcare, education, transit) | ×0.6 | Drop unless score is already very high. |
| Org profile = Consultant reselling | ×0.5 | Interesting but complicated; flag for Growth. |
| Population 100k–250k | ×0.9 | Tier-2 population per ICP doc. |
| Population 250k+ | ×1.0 | Tier-1 population. |
| Population 500k+ | ×1.1 | Already has +2 bonus, this is an additional nudge. |
| Department = City Manager / Mayor's Office / Innovation | ×1.1 | Steering department — fastest path. |
| Department = Engagement / Planning | ×1.0 | Default. |
| Department = Comms | ×0.95 | Supporting but vertical. |
| Department = other / unclear | ×0.9 | |
| Progressive/left-center political control (if identifiable) | ×1.05 | From ICP doc: slightly higher fit. |
| Conservative political control | ×1.0 | Default. |

Apply only modifiers you can verify from the source. If you can't verify, default to ×1.0.

## Worked example

> **Signal**: City of Lyon (FR) published a tender on BOAMP for a "plateforme de consultation citoyenne et budget participatif". Budget €180k over 2 years. Published 4 days ago. Also covered in La Gazette des Communes.

Scoring:
- Base: published tender = **10**
- Multi-source (BOAMP + Gazette): **+2**
- Population > 500k (Lyon ~520k): **+2**
- Tier 1 geography (France): **+2**
- Signal < 7 days: **+1**
- Budget ≥ €100k: **+2**
- Platform capability named (participatory budgeting): **+1**
- Subtotal: **20** (capped)

Fit modifier: local govt ×1.0, pop 500k+ ×1.1, department = Mayor's Office (if verified) ×1.1 → 20 × 1.1 × 1.1 ≈ 24, recap to 20.

**Final score: 20 — exceptional. DM Wietse immediately.**

## Selecting the top 10

1. Compute base + bonuses + modifier for each candidate.
2. Sort by final score descending.
3. If the #10 and #11 signals are tied, prefer the one with:
   - A more tender-like signal type (tender > budget > strategic plan > council > press)
   - A Tier-1 geography
   - More recent date
   - Platform capability explicitly named

4. If fewer than 10 candidates score ≥ 6 in a given week, write only the ones that qualify and flag the market thinness in the Tuesday-run log. Don't pad with weak signals.
