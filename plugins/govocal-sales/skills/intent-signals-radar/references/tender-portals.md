# Tender and procurement portals by region

This is the scan list. Each run, hit these portals in parallel (one subagent per region). Search with the English keyword list translated into each portal's working language.

## Universal keyword set (translate per portal)

**Primary (must match)**
- citizen participation / participation citoyenne / burgerparticipatie / Bürgerbeteiligung / participación ciudadana / partecipazione cittadina
- public consultation platform / plateforme de consultation / inspraakplatform / Beteiligungsplattform
- digital engagement / engagement numérique / digitale Bürgerbeteiligung
- participatory budget / budget participatif / burgerbegroting / Bürgerhaushalt / presupuesto participativo
- e-participation / participation numérique / E-Partizipation

**Secondary (bonus signal)**
- citizens' assembly / assemblée citoyenne / burgerberaad / Bürgerrat
- ideation platform / plateforme d'idéation
- deliberative democracy / démocratie délibérative
- co-creation platform / co-création
- online consultation / consultation en ligne
- open government / gouvernement ouvert / open overheid

**Negative filters** (to avoid noise — deprioritize hits that ONLY contain these)
- "patient engagement" (healthcare)
- "employee engagement" (HR)
- "customer engagement" (CRM)
- "SEO" / "marketing engagement"

## EU-wide

### TED (Tenders Electronic Daily) — ted.europa.eu
The master source for all EU member-state tenders above threshold. Use the advanced search:
- CPV codes: 72000000-5 (IT services), 79000000-4 (Business services), 72320000-4 (Database services), 79416100-4 (PR services)
- Place of performance: filter by EU country
- Publication date: last 14 days

## Budget & strategic plan discovery (non-tender source)

Not every signal lives in a tender portal. City budget and multi-year strategic plan documents often disclose intent months before a tender publishes. Cast a wider net:

- Each city's `/budget`, `/strategic-plan`, `/city-council` pages
- National open-data portals: data.gouv.fr, overheid.nl, data.gov.uk, data.gov, open.canada.ca
- **Google advanced queries**: `site:[city-domain] (budget OR "strategic plan" OR "plan stratégique") ("participation" OR "engagement")`
- Archives of council meeting agendas where budgets are debated
