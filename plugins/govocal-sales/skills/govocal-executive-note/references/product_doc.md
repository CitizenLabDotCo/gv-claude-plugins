## Stats to handle with care (validate before shipping)

- "Go Vocal in numbers" block (CSAT 4.7/5, 40 countries, 640+ governments, People Powered 100/100, 27,100 projects, monthly active admins, community members) — pull the **current** figures from the product doc's *Our impact in numbers*; they drift between versions.

## Citation rule

When a feature appears in the note, it must trace to the product doc. If a needed feature is **not** in the doc, do not write it from memory — surface it to the user: "X isn't in the current product doc; confirm wording or I'll leave it out."
