# EN-NorthAm terminology & voice (the master locale)

> Source: Modular Proposal Template V12 (EN-NorthAm master), read in full 2026-07-03.

## Standard terms

- **"Community members"** — the default word for residents/citizens (impact numbers,
  method taglines, GDPR passages). "Participants", "residents", "users", "visitors" have
  role-specific meanings. "Citizens" is avoided except inside the variable "citizen
  participation platform".
- Client institution: **city / local government / organization**.
- Platform descriptor is a **template variable**: *citizen participation platform /
  community engagement platform / digital engagement platform* — mirror the prospect's own
  language from the RFP.
- Product spine vocabulary: **projects → phases → participation methods**; **inputs/ideas**
  (renamable per project); **official updates**; **statuses**; **front office / back
  office**; **smart groups**; **participation ladder**; **closing the feedback loop**;
  **culture of participation**; **participation success plan**; **GovSuccess**;
  "Government Success Manager", "Government Support Specialist", "dedicated participation team".
- Branded names verbatim: 360 Input, FormSync, ECHO by Dembrane, Input IQ, Common Ground,
  Perspectives, Sensemaking, Auto-Insights, Community Monitor, Inspiration Hub, Content
  Builder, Page Builder, Form Builder, Report Builder, Input Manager, Participation Feed,
  Management Feed, Representativeness Dashboard, Phase Dashboards, FIN (support chatbot),
  Konveio, Weglot, Geospatial Suite.
- Method aliases: Approval Voting = "One Vote per Option"; Cumulative = "Multiple Votes
  per Option" = dot-voting; Participatory Budgeting = "Basket Exercise" = "Budget Game";
  Ideation = "Public Forum".
- Legacy residue: support/developer links still say citizenlab.co; Go Vocal = rebrand of
  CitizenLab.

## Voice & style (apply when writing proposal/RFP prose)

- **Pain → false choice → our answer** structure for differentiators ("Traditional
  platforms force you to choose: either lose these voices entirely or spend countless
  hours manually transcribing…").
- **Concrete micro-scenarios**: "a nurse on a healthcare question, a local housing officer
  on a planning consultation"; "What do community members under 30 think about transit?"
- **Quantified proof points** attached to claims: 95% OCR accuracy, 2/3 mobile, 27
  languages, 30 features in 2025, 8–10-week Shape Up cycles, >85% test coverage, 99.9%
  uptime, ~3h support response, 640+ governments, People Powered 100/100.
- Second person, benefits over mechanics; bold key phrases tied to requirements; italic
  one-line taglines under method umbrellas; captions as "*Back-office – X*" / "*Front-office – X*".
- AI reassurance framing: evidence-linked summaries, human corrections, "the human
  maintains firm control"; "nothing goes public until you say so".
- Partnership voice: named people, not tiers — "your participation expert", "we design
  your first projects together".
- Depth calibration: critical differentiators 3–5 paragraphs; workflow features 2–3;
  standard functionality 1; table stakes 1–2 sentences on compliance/spec.
