## BE-vs-NL divergences (the point of this file)

- **GDPR, never AVG** — Flanders says GDPR; the Netherlands says AVG. Clearest split.
- Product/marketing voice says **burger(s)** ("burgerparticipatie", "burgervoorstellen");
  the Flemish case studies say **inwoners** (+ Gentenaars, Leuvenaars) — the warmer
  register residents actually hear. For NL-NL swap most "burger" → "inwoner".
- Belgian institutional vocabulary: **gemeenteraad** (proposals reaching threshold go "op
  de agenda van de gemeenteraad"), stadsbestuur/het bestuur, lokale overheden, gemeenten
  en steden in Vlaanderen, wijk/buurt/gehucht, **wijkbudget/burgerbudget**, gelote
  wijkpanels, inspraakreacties, **premies** (BE; NL-NL says subsidies). The doc avoids
  schepen/schepencollege altitude.
- Belgian eID stack: **CSAM / ItsMe / MijnBurgerprofiel** (paid add-on) — NL-NL equivalent
  DigiD is absent from the product's list.
- **"Denk Mee"** is the signature Flemish participation CTA (Denk Mee Lokeren,
  denkmee.sint-truiden.be); "meedenken" as engagement idiom.
- **licentiekost** ("kost" as noun = Belgian Dutch; NL-NL: licentiekosten); btw 21%;
  "officiële Belgische feestdagen" for support hours; AnySurfer (Belgian a11y label)
  resonates locally.
- Address: client org = **jullie**, reader = je/jouw (acceptable in Flemish B2G), but the
  doc slips into u/uw mid-paragraph — pick one register per audience layer and enforce.

|---|---|
| workflow steps | Configureer › Betrek › Beheer › Beslis (Configureren/Betrekken/Beheren/Beslissen) |
| IAP2 ladder | Informeren, Consulteren, Betrekken, Co-creëren, Co-beslissen |
