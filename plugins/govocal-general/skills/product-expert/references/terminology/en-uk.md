> updated 2026-07-02, read in full 2026-07-03. UK localization is done **inline**, not via

## Term choices

| Concept | EN-NorthAm | EN-UK usage |
|---|---|---|
| Client institution | city / local government / organization | **council**, **local authority**, borough ("local governments" kept for global stats) |
| People (primary) | community members | **community members** (retained — NOT globally swapped to "residents"; "residents" appears only occasionally) |
| Platform descriptor | community engagement platform | variable per tender: digital engagement platform / community engagement platform / participation platform / "360° engagement hub" |
| Procurement | RFP | **tender** leads; \[tender/RFP/bid\] is a pick-one variable |
| Success role | Customer Success Manager | **Government Success Manager**, IAP2-certified (UK bio: strategic engagement advisor to the council) |
| UK social proof | US cities | London Borough of Newham, Durham County, Wigan Borough Council |
| Security certifier | ISO 27001 | ISO 27001 **certified by BSI** (bsigroup.com verification link) — UK-credible signal |
| Hosting | US regions | **UK (London) AWS region** available — foreground data residency for UK tenders |
| Data protection | GDPR | doc says "GDPR" only — **never "UK GDPR" / DPA 2018**; writer must bridge this gap |
| Licence | license | **licence** (noun) — doc is inconsistent; UK convention: licence (noun), license (verb) |

Participation-method names are unchanged (Ideation, Proposals, Participatory Budgeting aka
"Basket Exercise"/"Budget Game", Voting & Prioritisation with Approval="One Vote per
Option" and Cumulative="Multiple Votes per Option"/dotvoting, Polls, Surveys, Common
Ground, Perspectives, Community Monitor, Workshops, Document annotation/Konveio, FormSync,
ECHO by Dembrane, 360 Input).

## Spelling

-ise family applied (personalised, organisation, customise, prioritisation, anonymised,
authorisation, digitise…) **but the sweep is incomplete** — see hazards.

## Tone & register

Formal, warm, letter-like: exec summary is a signed CEO letter ("Dear <> team … Sincerely,
Wietse Van Ransbeeck"). Partnership language ("hand-in-hand", "culture of participation").
Values-forward material that lands in UK procurement: local democracy, SDG 16.7, B Corp,
gender-balanced leadership, People Powered #1 ranking, IAP2 spectrum framing.

## ⚠ Known hazards in the UK doc (fix per proposal)

- **"citisen"** — an over-applied z→s conversion in the variables ("citisen participation
  platform", "Citisen Engagement" award name). Always "citizen".
- Residual Americanisms: analyze, color, behavior, neighborhood, program, favorite.
- Typos: "Entreprise" (plan name), "visualiseparticipation".
- **Pricing is in EUR with continental formatting ("€ 1.000,00")** — convert to GBP/UK
  formatting for UK tenders.
- Support hours quoted CET/CT, not GMT/BST; legacy `support@citizenlab.co` mailtos.
- No G-Cloud/framework, Cyber Essentials, social value, or UK-GDPR/DPA-2018 sections exist
  — the writer adds these when a tender requires them.
- Duplicated accessibility paragraph; "Recent releases" heading vs ToC mismatch; two
  near-identical pricing example tables (keep one).
- UK doc trails the EN master: V10–V12 changes are authored centrally and ported; check the
  master's changelog for anything newer than the UK port.

**Pre-submission sweep is mandatory**: spelling, currency, timezone, citisen→citizen,
council names in template scaffolding ("City of XXX", "City Project Team", "reference city 1").
