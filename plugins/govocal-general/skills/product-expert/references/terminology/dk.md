## Caveats for proposal writing

- Danish KB is the most anglicized locale — decide deliberately what stays English
  (Sensemaking, FormSync, ECHO, 360 Input yes; "Platform Admin"/"Folder Manager" no —
  localize roles as platformadministrator / mappeadministrator or similar).
- Fix before quoting: NemID→MitID, "Deltagende Budgettering"→borgerbudget,
  "Ineligibel"→ikke gyldigt/afvist-style phrasing, fortrolighedspolitik→privatlivspolitik.
- DA listing lacks the Common Ground article (ES has it) and one title is untranslated
  ("Community Monitoring/Resident Satisfaction Survey") — check EN for those features.
