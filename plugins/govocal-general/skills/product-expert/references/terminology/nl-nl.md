## Key lexical choices (respect these)

- **inwoner(s)** is the dominant word for the public; *burger(s)* secondary (mission/ladder
  contexts, "burgervoorstellen"). The burger/inwoner register is a per-tender variable —
  guidelines name "burgerparticipatieplatform vs community engagement platform".
- **participatieplatform** = default descriptor; **participatiecultuur** recurring frame.
- **je/jullie, not u** — the client org is "jullie", the reader "je". Normalize stray "u".
- Tender vocabulary: **aanbesteding, aanbestedende dienst, offerteaanvraag, vereisten/eisen,
  Managementsamenvatting, Prijsvoorstel**, btw 21%, "€18.000,00" formatting.

## ⚠ Known hazards in the NL doc (fix per proposal)

- **Structure trails the EN master**: still the 4-step Inrichten/Betrekken/Beheren/Beslissen
  (EN V13 is restructuring to …Analyze/Act). Content synced through V12 (Raakvlakken, ECHO,
  webhooks all present).
- Stale bits: roadmap says "2024"; example license period 2024–2025; "600" vs "640+" vs
  "+500" government counts disagree.
- References claim "70+ gemeenten in Nederland" but all three written cases are **Belgian**
  (Lokeren, Leuven, Gent) — swap in Dutch references for NL tenders.
- MT artifacts: "robijn applicatie" (= Ruby application), "gepubliceerdd", "Goverment
  Succes Manager", untranslated fragments ("Advisory session of choice", "Data export").
- A few "u" slips in an otherwise je/jullie doc.
