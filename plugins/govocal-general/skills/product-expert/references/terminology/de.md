# DACH (DE) terminology & conventions

> Source: DACH Modular Proposal Template (Google Doc `1akm1c8-aiFfSjwE7gl8J9ss-qXf1w0wd`),
> updated 2026-06-08, read in full 2026-07-03.

## Key lexical choices (respect these)

- **Beteiligung- compounds dominate**: Beteiligungsplattform, Beteiligungsmethode,
  Beteiligungsprojekt, Beteiligungskultur. *Partizipation-* variants are the rarer marked
  alternative. "Bürger\*innenbeteiligung" (starred) is more common than "Bürgerbeteiligung"
  in body text.
- **Bürger\*innen** = the dominant word for residents-as-civic-actors; Einwohner\*innen /
  Bewohner\*innen / Anwohnende for residents-of-a-place; **die Verwaltung** = the default
  word for the client organization; **die Öffentlichkeit** very frequent; **Vorhaben** as
  an elegant synonym for a project/initiative.
- Rich local geography vocabulary: Nachbarschaft, Stadtteil, Bezirk, Ortsteil, Viertel, Quartier.

## Conventions

- **Sie, always** for the client; participants in third person (die Teilnehmenden).
- **Gendersternchen is the house style** (Bürger\*innen, Nutzer\*innen…); substantivized
  participles (Teilnehmende, Mitarbeitende) as the second gender-neutral strategy.
  Plain-masculine passages are a marker of recently machine-translated text — fix them.
- German typographic quotes „…"; decimal comma ("99,9 %"), thin-space thousands ("27 100").
- DACH staff use the hyphenated **go-vocal.de** email domain.

## ⚠ Known hazards in the DE doc (fix per proposal)

- Untranslated EN paragraph in "Zukünftige Roadmap"; roadmap table has a duplicated cell
  and a lost row.
- Duplicated sections (Anonyme Teilnahme ×2, Sichtbarkeit repeated, Optionsanalyse
  double-paragraph); truncated bullet lists ("Diese Projekte:" with no bullets);
  mislabeled figure ("Darstellung: FormSync" under Projektansicht).
- Typos: "Singe-Sign-On", "Platform-administartor\*innen", "Klassifizeriung",
  "Cookierechtlinie", truncated phone number, mojibake "ð±".
- No pricing tables (plan names only via support tiers); no standalone Polls or
  Volunteering sections.
- Legacy citizenlab.co links/mailtos.
