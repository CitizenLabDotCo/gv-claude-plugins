## Legislation & Policy Tracking

New or updated legislation on participation and consultation is extremely high-signal — it directly creates market demand for Go Vocal's platform. Always flag these prominently.

### What to watch for
- **Mandatory consultation laws**: legislation requiring governments to consult citizens (e.g., France's participation mandates for urban planning, Belgium's regional consultation requirements)
- **Participatory budgeting mandates**: laws requiring PB at municipal or regional level
- **Digital democracy frameworks**: EU or national-level policies on e-participation, digital public infrastructure for engagement
- **Citizens' assembly institutionalization**: laws embedding deliberative processes into governance (e.g., Brussels' mixed commissions, Ostbelgien's permanent citizens' council)
- **Right to petition / initiative reforms**: changes that expand or digitize citizen initiative rights
- **EU-level developments**: European Citizens' Initiative updates, Conference on the Future of Europe follow-ups, EU democracy action plans
