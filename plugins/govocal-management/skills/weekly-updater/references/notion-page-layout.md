subpages below (sidebar)" — Jeroen explicitly asked not to repeat the
pointer in prose.

### Notes on the canonical page body

- Use `---` horizontal rules between sections. They render as a thin divider
  in Notion and make the page scannable.
- Section headings are `## 1 | …` through `## 5 | …`. Use the pipe `|`
  literally, with single spaces around it — matches the existing Go Vocal
  convention in the Monthly Sales Meeting template.
- Do not add an executive summary at the top. Jeroen reads the sections
  directly.
- KPI block has no sub-header; just the four bullet lines from cfo-revenues.
- Section 2 carries both customer-sourced signal streams (VoC + Escalations)
  because they're both about what's happening in the customer world.
- Section 5 carries Misalignment because it informs Wietse's priority-setting
  rather than reporting a discrete win or loss.

## Archive subpage

Title: **Archive** (NO emoji in the title; icon provides it.)
Parent: canonical page
Icon: `🗂️`

This subpage is long-lived — created once, then on each weekly run it gets
one new child appended. Its own body stays minimal:

```markdown
*Archive of prior Weekly Updates. Each child subpage is a full snapshot of
the week's generic update + its three Read-more subpages.*

Children (newest first):
- Week of <most-recent-prior>
- Week of <week before>
- …
```

The Children list is optional — Notion already shows children in the sidebar.
If you maintain it, update it each week; otherwise delete that paragraph.

### Archive child — Week of <YYYY-MM-DD>

Title: **Week of <YYYY-MM-DD>** (use the Monday of the archived week, not
today)
Parent: `🗂️ Archive`
Icon: `📅`

Body = the full content of the canonical generic update from the week you're
archiving, copied as-is. The three Read-more subpages from that week become
children of this archive page (moved, not duplicated).

## Dates, timezones, number formatting

- All week boundaries are **Monday 00:00 → Sunday 23:59 Europe/Brussels**.
- `Week of` date = the Monday of the reporting week, format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- Preparation timestamp format: `YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm CET` (24-hour).
- Currency: € symbol, comma thousands, rounded to the euro under €10k and to
  the nearest €100 above (matches cfo-revenues convention).
- Percentages: one decimal, trailing `%`.

## Links — how to format

- PlanHat account link (from voc-gs-weekly-digest): `[Account
  Name](https://app.planhat.com/profile/<id>)` — already produced by that
  skill, pass through as-is.
- Slack thread link (from misalignment-radar or escalations): full
  `https://govocal.slack.com/archives/<CID>/p<TS>` URL — pass through.
- Notion subpage links: use the shortened form returned by
  `notion-create-pages` (page URL without the workspace slug is fine).
- Fireflies timestamp links: pass through as-is from voc-gs-weekly-digest
  and escalations skill.
